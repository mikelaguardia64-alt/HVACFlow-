# HVACFlow+ First-Customer Sales Playbook

## Offer
HVACFlow+ — $497/month.

Core promise:
Turn missed calls and new leads into booked jobs.

Do not promise a specific number of booked jobs or a guaranteed return. Sell the workflow and the business problem it addresses.

## Ideal customer profile
Start with owner-led or small-to-mid-sized HVAC companies that:
- receive inbound calls and web leads;
- have a field-service team;
- have a sales/office function;
- acknowledge missed calls or slow lead response as a problem;
- can make a $497/month software decision without a long procurement process.

## 15-minute demo
1. Ask: “How are new calls and web leads handled today?”
2. Ask: “What happens when someone calls and nobody answers?”
3. Ask: “Who owns follow-up after the first contact?”
4. Show the HVACFlow+ lead dashboard.
5. Show missed-call recovery and SMS workflow.
6. Show appointment workflow and CRM connection.
7. Show the $497/month plan.
8. Ask: “If this solved the follow-up gap you described, would you want to start?”

## Discovery questions
- How many inbound calls do you receive in a typical week?
- What happens after a missed call?
- How quickly does someone follow up on a new web lead?
- Where do you track prospects?
- How are appointments scheduled?
- Who is responsible for follow-up?
- What happens to leads that do not answer the first callback?

## Objection handling
**“We already have a CRM.”**
That makes sense. HVACFlow+ is focused on the HVAC lead-response and missed-call workflow. The question is whether your current system is actually closing the response gap.

**“$497 is too much.”**
Understood. Before discussing price, let’s quantify the problem. If there is no meaningful missed-call or follow-up gap, HVACFlow+ may not be a fit.

**“Send me information.”**
Absolutely. I’ll send the overview. If it looks relevant, we can do a 15-minute walkthrough.

**“I need to think about it.”**
Of course. What part do you want to think through — the workflow, implementation, or price?

## Weekly prospecting target
Start manually:
- 25 new HVAC prospects researched
- 25 personalized first contacts
- 10 follow-ups
- 3–5 demos targeted

Track every contact in Close CRM. Measure responses, demos, trials/checkouts, and paid conversions before increasing volume.
